Festivals and events

Bellydance, tap, Indian dance and special cultural evenings — workshops, showcases, performances and community, all under one roof.

  1. 7–9 May2027

    Gent India Dans Festival

    Three days of Indian dance every May — workshops, a gala showcase and an opening party that runs until late.

    4 editions since 2023Curated by Swapnil Dagliya
  2. Bellydance · Raqs Sharqi

    Leylet Raqs

    Ghent's international Raqs Sharqi festival — workshops, live music and a galashow, in the heart of the city.

    4 editions since 2023Curated by Lenka Badriyah
  3. Tap dance

    Ghent Tap Festival

    Two full days of tap — workshops across every level and an evening performance that fills the Shoonya stage.

    4 editions since 2023Hosted by Tapdance Promotion

Shoonya Dance Centre

These festivals and dance events are hosted at Shoonya Dance Centre, Stapelplein 41, 9000 Gent.
