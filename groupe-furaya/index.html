Groupe Furaya

One night of Congolese dance: first on stage, then with you on the floor.

Groupe Furaya brings together dance, singing, percussion and balafon in a style that moves between traditional and contemporary forms. The performance draws on rhythms and traditional songs from the Democratic Republic of Congo, carried by live instruments including tam-tams, lokole, maracas, balafon and other percussion.

The evening is built as a performance first: a 70-minute stage work with dancers, singers and musicians. After a short reset, three artists from the group lead short initiations so the audience can experience the rhythms through movement.

You can buy a ticket for the 19:00 performance, or simply arrive at 20:30 for the initiations.

FAQ

Practical questions

Is this a workshop or a performance?

The main event is a performance. The three short initiation classes happen after the performance.

Can I come only for the free initiations?

Yes. Doors open again at 20:30 and the initiations are free; you do not need a ticket for the 19:00 performance.

Do I need dance experience?

No. Each initiation is a 15-minute introduction taught from zero.

What languages will be used?

Joseph will teach in a mixed-language format, Bawayi in French, and Awa in English and French.

Is there a capacity limit for the free initiations?

Shoonya's room holds 90 people. Entry to the 20:30 initiations depends on safe room capacity on the night.

What should I wear?

Wear comfortable clothes you can move in. You can dance barefoot or in clean indoor dance shoes.

Where is Shoonya?

Shoonya Dance Centre is at Stapelplein 41, 9000 Gent, a short walk from Gent-Dampoort station.
