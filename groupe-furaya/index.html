From the floor

The kind of rhythm you step into at 20:30.

A short loop from Groupe Furaya: simple steps, call-and-response energy, and the room moving together. It plays muted here; turn the sound on if you want the live rhythm. Just arrive at 20:30; no registration needed.

Groupe Furaya

Congolese dance, song and live rhythm — made close enough to step into.

Groupe Furaya brings together dance, singing, percussion and balafon in a style that moves between traditional and contemporary forms. The performance draws on rhythms and traditional songs from the Democratic Republic of Congo, carried by live instruments including tam-tams, lokole, maracas and balafon.

There is also a direct Shoonya connection: Joseph Saidi Simako — who teaches African Dance, Congolese dance and Congolese rumba at Shoonya Dance Centre — is part of Groupe Furaya and leads the modern Ndombolo initiation.

A few Shoonya Dance Centre students will also take part in the performance.

FAQ

Practical questions

Can I come only for the free initiations?

Yes. Doors open again at 20:30 and the initiations are free. You do not need a performance ticket or a separate registration.

Are the performance and initiations in the same place?

They are in the same Shoonya building at Stapelplein 41. The performance is in the Shoonya Theatre; the initiations are in Studio Ananta on the ground floor.

Do I need dance experience?

No. Each initiation is a 15-minute introduction taught from zero.

What languages will be used?

Joseph will teach in a mixed-language format, Bawayi in French, and Awa in English and French.

What should I wear?

Wear comfortable clothes you can move in. You can dance barefoot or in clean indoor dance shoes.
